# Research and creative lane

For research, define the question and date boundary. Prefer primary or first-party sources, open the source rather than relying on snippets, compare versions and units, record source URLs, and qualify unsupported claims. Do not treat generated content or popularity as proof.

For UX/UI, model important flows as states and transitions. Every control needs an accessible name, visible focus/pressed/disabled/loading/success/error states, feedback, recovery, keyboard or touch support, contrast, responsive behavior, localization, and reduced-motion behavior where relevant.

For image/video/audio, define the brief, dimensions, duration, codec or format, rights/provenance, and acceptance criteria before creation. Review the actual artifact. Distinguish generated, edited, simulated, and measured results. Do not claim native resolution, accessibility, or real-provider behavior from appearance alone.

For long context, load only relevant files, preserve claim-level evidence, and write durable handoffs instead of relying on hidden conversation state.

**Image edits.** State the edit and the invariants (logo, exact text, untouched regions) first. Check rights and provenance, use deterministic rendering for exact text, inspect the real output size and artifacts, and never claim pixel-exact preservation without a comparison.

**Video.** Before generating, write the brief, storyboard or shot plan, a continuity record for recurring characters, and the rights for assets and music. Review captions, audio, and the exported file; label what was generated versus verified.

**People-related OSINT.** Trace each claim to primary or independent sources, separate where an artifact came from from whether the claim is true, keep contradictions visible, minimize personal data, and do not identify or expose private individuals.

**Context compaction.** Keep the goal, constraints, decisions, current state, evidence pointers, exact critical identifiers, authorization gates, pending failures, and unknowns. Check that nothing critical was lost instead of assuming the summary is faithful.
